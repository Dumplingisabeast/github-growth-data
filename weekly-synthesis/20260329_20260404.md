## 🔥 Trend 1: The Meta-Harness & Orchestration Layer
**12+ repos in Top 50**

The most significant organic growth comes from tools that act as a "wrapper" or "harness" for AI agents. These tools provide the memory, security guardrails, and toolsets that raw agents lack.

*   **`affaan-m/everything-claude-code` (3,339★)**: A performance optimization system that works across multiple agents including Codex and Cursor.
*   **`Gitlawb/openclaude` (2,753★)**: A truly multi-provider CLI that routes to 200+ models via OpenAI-compatible APIs. This represents the "unbundling" of the agent UI from the underlying model.
*   **`paperclipai/paperclip` (1,513★)**: Orchestrating multiple agents into structured organizations (the "zero-human company" concept).
*   **`bytedance/deer-flow` (1,023★)**: ByteDance's entry into the SuperAgent harness space, focusing on long-horizon tasks and memory.

## 🔥 Trend 2: Foundational CLI Infrastructure
**3 repos in Top 10**

As AI agents increasingly inhabit the terminal, the demand for high-performance terminal UI (TUI) infrastructure is exploding.

*   **`chenglou/pretext` (5,184★)**: Ranked #3. A foundational library for text measurement and layout. Its massive growth indicates that the next generation of AI agents will feature highly sophisticated terminal interfaces.
*   **`microsoft/VibeVoice` (1,568★)**: Open-source frontier voice AI. This suggests that the "Terminal Agent" is rapidly moving toward multi-modal (voice + text) interaction.
