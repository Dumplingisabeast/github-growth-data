# Weekly Synthesis: March 29 – April 4, 2026

## Executive Summary: The Rise of the AI "Distribution"

This week marks a major inflection point in the AI development ecosystem. While the official release of **Claude Code** by Anthropic was the primary catalyst, the resulting data is heavily skewed by a massive source code leak. Over **25% of the stars** in this week's Top 100 were captured by "leak clones" and ports of the proprietary tool.

The real organic trend, however, is the shift toward **opinionated agent distributions**. Developers are no longer just using agents; they are curating and sharing massive toolkits, frameworks, and "standardized" configurations that transform a raw agent into a specialized production system.

## 🔥 Trend 1: The "Everything" Bundle — Curated Agent Distributions
**~12 repos in Top 50**

The dominant organic pattern this week is the creation of "distributions" — comprehensive packages that bundle everything a developer needs to make an agent useful.

*   **`affaan-m/everything-claude-code` (3,339★)**: The "Swiss Army Knife" of the week. This is a massive toolkit of production-ready agents, skills, hooks, and commands. While its tagline mentions performance optimization, its primary value is as a single source of truth for agent configurations across Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.
*   **`obra/superpowers` (2,243★)**: A formal skills framework and methodology that treats agentic development as a disciplined engineering practice.
*   **`luongnv89/claude-howto` (2,042★)**: An example-driven visual guide with copy-paste templates, moving the ecosystem from "what is this?" to "how do I use it?"
*   **`garrytan/gstack` (1,546★)**: A productized version of a top CEO's exact agent setup, featuring 23 specialized tool personas.
*   **`shanraisshan/claude-code-best-practice` (1,044★)**: A community-curated repository for distilling collective knowledge into repeatable patterns.
